list of course 

Introduction to Healthcare Management:

    Overview of healthcare systems and organizational structures.
    Principles of healthcare administration and leadership.
    Healthcare policy and regulatory frameworks.
    Financial management in healthcare settings.

Medical Terminology:

    Introduction to the language of medicine.
    Understanding medical prefixes, suffixes, and root words.
    Common medical abbreviations and acronyms.
    Interpretation of medical records and documentation.

Clinical Skills Training:

    Basic patient assessment techniques (vital signs, history taking, physical examination).
    Infection control and hygiene practices.
    Wound care and dressing techniques.
    Medication administration and dosage calculations.

Healthcare Ethics and Law:

    Ethical principles in healthcare decision-making.
    Patient rights and confidentiality.
    Legal issues in healthcare practice (e.g., informed consent, malpractice).
    Ethical dilemmas in medical research and treatment.

Health Information Management:

    Introduction to electronic health records (EHR) systems.
    Data collection, storage, and retrieval in healthcare settings.
    Privacy and security considerations in health information management.
    Health informatics and technology trends in healthcare.

Patient Education and Communication:

    Strategies for effective patient communication.
    Health literacy and patient empowerment.
    Counseling techniques for promoting behavior change.
    Providing culturally competent care.

Chronic Disease Management:

    Overview of common chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, asthma, etc.).
    Disease prevention and health promotion strategies.
    Self-management skills for patients with chronic illnesses.
    Collaborative care approaches involving healthcare teams.

Emergency Response and First Aid:

    Basic life support (CPR) techniques.
    First aid for common medical emergencies (choking, heart attack, stroke, etc.).
    Trauma assessment and management.
    Disaster preparedness and response protocols.
