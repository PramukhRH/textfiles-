There are several consultancies in Bangalore that specialize in study-abroad guidance and applications. Here are some well-regarded options:

Y-Axis: Provides comprehensive services, from test prep to visa assistance, and has a strong presence in study-abroad applications.

Edwise International: Known for having specialized counselors for different countries and offering end-to-end services, including visa and accommodation support.

The Chopras (TC Global): Offers career counseling and end-to-end application support, including test preparation and visa guidance.

IDP Education: IDP is a global organization partnered with several top universities, offering assistance from university selection to post-arrival support.

Imperial Overseas: Known for personalized counseling, particularly for students aiming to study in the U.S.
