To prepare for aptitude tests, follow these strategies:

1. Understand the Aptitude Test Structure
Common sections in aptitude tests:
Quantitative Ability (QA): Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, number systems, percentages, ratios, etc.
Logical Reasoning (LR): Number series, analogies, puzzles, seating arrangement, coding-decoding, etc.
Verbal Ability (VA): Reading comprehension, synonyms-antonyms, sentence correction, and vocabulary.
Data Interpretation (DI): Graphs, tables, pie charts, bar charts, etc.
Verbal and Analytical Reasoning: Data sufficiency, logical deduction.
2. Use Quality Study Material
Books:
R.S. Aggarwal’s "Quantitative Aptitude"
Arun Sharma’s "How to Prepare for Data Interpretation"
K. Kundan’s "Logical Reasoning"
"Word Power Made Easy" by Norman Lewis (for vocabulary).
Online Platforms:
Practice on websites like IndiaBix, Testbook, TalentSprint, PrepInsta, and M4Maths.
3. Make a Study Plan
Break your preparation into manageable topics.
Focus on one topic each day.
Allocate time for practice and revision.
Prioritize weaker sections but keep practicing your strengths too.
4. Learn Shortcuts & Tricks
Speed and Accuracy: Work on improving your speed and accuracy using shortcuts for mental math.
Learn tricks for fast calculation (e.g., squaring large numbers, quick percentage calculation).
5. Practice Regularly
Daily Practice: Consistency is key. Set a time for daily practice.
Mock Tests: Take full-length mock tests to simulate the actual exam environment. Track your performance and work on areas of improvement.
Time Management: Practice solving problems within a time limit to improve speed.
6. Focus on Weak Areas
Identify topics that you find difficult and devote more time to mastering them.
Review incorrect answers and understand the reasoning behind the correct solution.
7. Analyze Previous Years’ Papers
Understand the pattern and types of questions asked in previous aptitude tests.
This will help you get familiar with the common question formats and prepare accordingly.
8. Improve Mental Calculation
Work on improving your mental calculation abilities by practicing basic arithmetic operations without a calculator.
9. Take Breaks and Stay Motivated
Don’t burn out. Take short breaks between sessions to stay fresh.
Keep a positive mindset and celebrate small wins.
10. Mock Tests and Speed Practice
Take timed mock tests to practice solving problems under pressure.
Focus on eliminating unnecessary steps and optimizing your approach for quicker solutions.
